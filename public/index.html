01

Detection

A YOLOv8 detector trained on 5,000 annotated CubiCasa5k plans finds every wall, door and window. Raw boxes are noisy, so each opening is snapped to its nearest wall, re-oriented along that wall's axis, and de-duplicated — the detector often emits two boxes for one door.

Strays more than 45 px from any wall are discarded.

02

Scale

Pixels mean nothing until they mean metres. The scale is derived per plan — from printed dimension text when it can be read, otherwise by normalising the longest side to a realistic house width.

One scale flows through every stage after it, so walls stay 3 m tall whatever resolution the drawing arrived at.

03

Retrieval

Rather than asking a language model to invent a roof, the engine retrieves one. A library of 44 architectural compositions — opposing skillions with a clerestory band, cross-gables with a portico, stacked modernist volumes — is scored against the measured footprint's aspect, convexity, area and wing count.

The top candidates are sampled with temperature, so the same plan yields a different, still-appropriate house each run.

04

Geometry

The retrieved composition is executed by a constrained geometry engine. Hip and valley surfaces come from a distance field, so ridges follow any L, T or U footprint automatically. Openings are boolean-cut from the wall solids and refilled with framed joinery.

Every roof is then rasterised against its footprint. If more than 3 % is left uncovered, it is rebuilt as a hip — watertight by construction.
